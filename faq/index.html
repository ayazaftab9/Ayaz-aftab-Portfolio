Meta & Google AdsHow do you combat ad fatigue and rising CPAs on Meta and Google Ads?

Ad fatigue occurs when target audiences repeatedly see identical creatives, dropping click-through rates (CTR) and driving up CPMs. I run weekly 3–5 creative testing sprints pairing high-contrast visual hooks, unboxing reels, founder direct-to-camera angles, customer review carousels, and problem-solution product comparisons. By constantly injecting validated hook concepts into CBO scaling campaigns, we sustain sub-₹50 CPLs and high 4.0x+ ROAS.

Meta & Google AdsHow do you structure Google Performance Max (PMax) without cannibalizing branded Search?

Unoptimized Google PMax campaigns frequently waste spend bidding on your own brand keywords rather than discovering incremental customers. I isolate brand terms into a dedicated high-intent Search campaign with exact matching, add negative brand keyword lists to PMax asset groups, and configure customer acquisition value rules so Google algorithms prioritize net-new buyers instead of existing customers.
